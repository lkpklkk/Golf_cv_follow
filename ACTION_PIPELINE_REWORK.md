## 1. Problem

The live classifier reports `swing` while the golfer is idling. Two independent
root causes, both measured against the artifacts in `data/processed/`.

### 1.1 Leak A — the sampler accepts presence, not position

[`create_windows()`](action/generate_dataset.py#L535) seeds candidate window
starts across `[interval_start - window_sec, interval_end]` — a full 2.0 s of
leading pad and up to 2.0 s of trailing overhang, at a 0.15 s stride
([line 562](action/generate_dataset.py#L562)). The only filter is
`swing_overlap_threshold: 0.30`, which accepts a 2.0 s window containing as
little as **0.6 s of actual swing**.

Measured over the 1,147 generated swing samples:

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Samples containing out-of-interval frames | **70.7 %** |
| Samples with overlap ratio < 0.50 | **21.8 %** |
| Minimum overlap ratio | **0.307** |
| Worst-case idle inside a `swing` window | **1.39 s** of 2.0 s |
| Windows overlapping a labeled `other`/`walk` by > 0.2 s | **97** |

Those 97 exist because
[`assign_label_by_overlap()`](action/generate_dataset.py#L590) walks the
`priority` list and returns the first label clearing its threshold
([line 611](action/generate_dataset.py#L611)) — the competing label is never
weighed.

### 1.2 Leak B — the labels themselves are loose

Median labeled swing interval is **2.96 s** (p90 4.10 s, max 6.11 s). A real
swing is ~1.2–1.7 s, so even a 100 %-overlap window is likely dominated by
address, waggle, and post-shot standing. **Phase 1 does not fix this**; see
Phase 2 Stage A.

### 1.3 Amplifier — no idle negatives

`include_unlabeled_as_other: false` means unlabeled video generates no samples at
all. Of 63.2 labeled minutes, **only 7.6 min carries any label — 55.6 min is
unlabeled and unused.** `other` is the smallest class (426 samples) while an idle
pool ~28× the size of the entire swing corpus sits idle. Idle is simultaneously
**under-represented as a negative** and **over-represented inside `swing`**.

### 1.4 Why current metrics did not catch it

The test set is generated by the same contaminated windowing, so an idle-heavy
clip is labeled `swing` in the ground truth too. Predicting `swing` on it scores
as correct — held-out swing precision reads **0.955**. The eval and the training
data share the same defect. This is why Stage 4 is "watch it by eye" rather than
"check the metrics".

### 1.5 Compounding effect at inference

[`ActionSequenceBuffer._resample()`](action/live_buffer.py#L75) builds a
**trailing** window, so the swing slides through it front-to-back. At a 0.30
acceptance threshold the detector fires once ~0.6 s of swing has entered the
window tail and keeps firing until ~1.4 s **after** the swing ends. Hot duration
≈ `swing_duration + 0.8 s`, ~40 % of it post-shot idle.

### 1.6 Footage inventory

Relevant to every stage below:

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Total in `data/raw_videos/` | 79.4 min (59 files) |
| Labeled, not skipped | 46 videos / 63.2 min |
| **Labeled swing time** | **2.0 min — 3.2 %** |
| Unlabeled | 55.6 min (88 %) |
| Swing intervals | 40 (min 1.50 s, median 2.96 s, max 6.11 s) |

Swing footage is the scarce resource; idle footage is abundant. This asymmetry
justifies Phase 1's central trade: **discard ambiguous swing samples freely,
because clean negatives are cheap.**

## Stage 1 — Impact-event labeling mode

**Goal:** a small set of fresh footage labeled under a protocol that cannot
inherit the sampler's defects, for use as an eyeball test in Stage 4.

**Independent of Stages 2–3.** Can be done in parallel.

### 1.1 Why events, not intervals

Holdout footage is never trained on, so it never goes through window generation
and never needs intervals. A single impact frame per swing is enough, and impact
is visually unambiguous in a way that "when does a swing start" is not — that
ambiguity is the origin of Leak B (§1.2).

**Existing training annotations are NOT relabeled.** All 40 intervals in
`data/annotations/video_intervals.json` stay exactly as they are. Phase 2 Stage A
derives impacts from them automatically if needed.

### 1.2 Collect footage

Place new video in **`data/hold_out/`** (the directory as actually created; every
`data/holdout/` reference elsewhere in this document means this one).

| Requirement | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Rig and framing | **same as existing footage** | a different course or camera setup measures domain shift, not this fix |
| Session | different from training footage | existing 56 videos are session-correlated |
| Non-swing behaviour | **~20–30 min** | false-positive rate needs a denominator, and idle is the failing case |
| Real swings | 10–15 | recall sanity check only |

Non-swing behaviour should be *realistic*: walking between shots, standing,
waggling, addressing and backing off, bending to tee up, club selection, putting.

### 1.3 Exclude holdout from training — structurally

[`discover_videos()`](utils/annotations.py#L11) must never return holdout files.
`data/hold_out/` is outside `raw_video_dir` so it is excluded by default, but add
an explicit assertion in dataset generation that no holdout filename appears in
the output. A convention is not sufficient here — silently training on the
holdout would invalidate every subsequent measurement.

**Implemented:** `data.holdout_dir: "data/hold_out"` in
[`dataset_config.yaml`](dataset_config.yaml#L5) feeds
[`holdout_video_names()`](utils/annotations.py#L24), and
[`assert_no_holdout_videos()`](action/generate_dataset.py#L104) raises
`SystemExit` from `generate_pose_dataset()` — once on the discovered video list
and again on the sample metadata immediately before `metadata.json` is written.
Absent `holdout_dir`, or a missing directory, the check is inert.

### 1.4 Add the labeling mode

Extend [`utils/annotate_intervals.py`](utils/annotate_intervals.py) with an
event-labeling mode (e.g. `--events` flag). It reuses the existing playback,
seeking, and overlay machinery in `annotate_video()`; only the keybindings and
output format change.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `I` | mark current frame as **impact** |
| `P` | mark current frame as **practice swing** |
| `U` | delete last mark |
| `N` / `Q` | save and next / save and quit |

Also carried over from interval mode: `SPACE` play/pause, `[`/`]` speed,
`,`/`.` frame step, `A`/`D` ±5 s, `K` skip video. Unlike interval mode `A` has no
floor — marks are instants, so seeking back past one cannot leave a half-open
annotation.

Output `data/hold_out/impact_events.json`:

```json
[{"video": "IMG_5xxx.mp4",
  "fps": 59.94,
  "impacts_sec": [12.44, 38.91, 61.02],
  "practice_swings_sec": [22.10]}]
```

**Implemented:** `golf_cv_annotate --events` (optionally `--video-dir` / `--out`).
[`annotate_video_events()`](utils/annotate_intervals.py#L678) is a separate
playback loop reusing the seeking and timing helpers; interval mode is unchanged
apart from `main()` gaining argparse. Already-labeled videos prompt before
relabeling and preload their existing marks, so `U` can edit them. Marking the
same frame twice is rejected rather than duplicated.

### 1.5 Decide before labeling: should a practice swing fire?

For a cart-following system the answer is probably **no**, which makes practice
swings the hardest negative in the set and requires them marked separately. The
answer defines what counts as a false positive in Stage 4. Existing interval
labels do not distinguish them. **Record the decision in this document when made.**



> Decision: It should fire. Doesn't matter. (which would essentially mean I (the user) will label an impact point even for a practice swing, therefore it won't be marked as false positive)

**As labeled:** practice swings were in fact marked with `P`, not folded into `I`
— 9 of them, kept as a separate list. Confirmed by the user as correct. This is
compatible with the decision and strictly more informative, but it makes the
Stage 4 scoring rule explicit:

> **Stage 4 positives = `impacts_sec` ∪ `practice_swings_sec`.** A fire on a
> practice swing is an expected detection, not a false positive. Scoring against
> `impacts_sec` alone would charge 9 correct fires as false positives and make
> the fix look worse than it is.

## Stage 2 — Ignore band in the sampler

**Goal:** stop ambiguous windows from becoming *either* positives or negatives.

**Independent of Stage 1.** This is the most important change in Phase 1 — do
not skip it or reduce it to a config edit. Config alone is actively harmful; see
2.2.

### 2.1 What an ignore band is

Currently one threshold forces every window into a label, creating a cliff: a
window at 0.31 overlap becomes a confident `swing`, one at 0.29 becomes
something else. Same clip, opposite training signal. Three zones instead of two:

```
ratio ≥ swing_overlap_threshold   →  swing            (confident positive)
ratio ≤ swing_ignore_below        →  eligible as negative / background
in between                        →  EMIT NOTHING     ← the ignore band
```

Borrowed from anchor assignment in object detection (RPN / RetinaNet mark
IoU ≥ 0.7 positive, ≤ 0.3 negative, in-between *ignored* and excluded from the
loss). Forcing a label onto an ambiguous case injects noise that costs more than
the missing sample.

> **Ignore is not the same as negative.** A window overlapping a swing genuinely
> contains swing evidence. Training it as `other` teaches the model to suppress
> real swings — strictly worse than the contamination being fixed. The window
> must be **absent from the dataset**, not moved to the opposite class.

### 2.2 The hazard this exists to prevent

[`assign_label_by_overlap()`](action/generate_dataset.py#L611-L619) ends with:

```python
for label in priority:
    threshold = _overlap_threshold_for_label(label, dataset_config)
    ratio, source_interval = best_by_label.get(label, (0.0, None))
    if ratio >= threshold:
        return label, int(label_to_id[label]), source_interval

if dataset_config["windowing"].get("include_unlabeled_as_other", True):
    return "other", int(label_to_id["other"]), None    # ← everything lands here
return None, None, None
```

Raising `swing_overlap_threshold` **and** setting
`include_unlabeled_as_other: true` without this guard means a window that is
**69 % swing** fails the threshold, falls through every branch, and is labeled
**`other`**. That is the failure mode described in the callout above, introduced
by the very change meant to fix things. Stage 2 must land before Stage 3.

### 2.3 Implement the guard

In [`action/generate_dataset.py`](action/generate_dataset.py), inside
`assign_label_by_overlap()`, **after `best_by_label` is built and before the
priority loop**:

```python
if _is_ambiguous_window(best_by_label, dataset_config):
    return None, None, None
```

And a new private helper:

```python
def _is_ambiguous_window(best_by_label, dataset_config):
    """
    True when the window overlaps an action interval too much to be a clean
    negative but too little to be a confident positive. Such windows are
    dropped entirely rather than forced into a label.
    """
    for label, (ratio, _) in best_by_label.items():
        if label == "other":
            continue
        threshold = _overlap_threshold_for_label(label, dataset_config)
        ignore_below = _ignore_below_for_label(label, dataset_config)
        if ignore_below < ratio < threshold:
            return True
    return False


def _ignore_below_for_label(label, dataset_config):
    key = f"{label}_ignore_below"
    return float(dataset_config["windowing"].get(key, 0.0))
```

Notes:

- `other` is excluded from the check — it is the background class, and a partial
  overlap with an `other` interval is still idle either way.
- Default `0.0` keeps behaviour unchanged when the new keys are absent, so the
  change is inert until Stage 3 sets them.
- Applies to `walk` as well as `swing`: a 40 %-walk window that fails walk's
  threshold should not silently become `other`.

### 2.4 Cap background windows

Setting `include_unlabeled_as_other: true` in Stage 3 sweeps the **entire** 63
minutes of footage instead of only the padded regions around labeled intervals.
Two consequences:

1. **Pose inference cost.** Unique frames requiring YOLO goes from ~13 k to
   ~57 k (63 min × 60 × 15 fps). Roughly 4× current generation time.
2. **Class balance.** `other` would swamp the dataset.

Note that raising `other_stride_sec` does *not* help much — once coverage is
continuous, the union of sampled frames is essentially every frame regardless of
stride. The fix is to not cover everything.

[`create_windows()`](action/generate_dataset.py#L535) currently accumulates both
the unlabeled sweep and the per-interval ranges into a **single**
`candidate_ticks` set, so the two cannot be capped independently as written.
Split them first:

```python
background_ticks = set()
interval_ticks = set()

if include_unlabeled_as_other:
    other_stride = float(dataset_config["windowing"]["other_stride_sec"])
    _add_window_start_range(background_ticks, 0.0, max_start, other_stride, target_fps)

for interval in intervals:
    ...
    _add_window_start_range(interval_ticks, start, end, stride, target_fps)

# Cap only the background sweep; interval-derived ticks are never dropped.
background_ticks -= interval_ticks
max_background = dataset_config["windowing"].get("max_background_windows")
if max_background is not None and len(background_ticks) > int(max_background):
    rng = random.Random(dataset_config["sampling"].get("timestamp_jitter_seed", 42))
    background_ticks = set(rng.sample(sorted(background_ticks), int(max_background)))

candidate_ticks = interval_ticks | background_ticks
```

Two details that matter:

- **Subtract `interval_ticks` before capping**, or a tick that belongs to both
  sets could be removed by the background cap and lose a labeled window.
- **Sort before `rng.sample`.** Iteration order of a `set` is not stable across
  runs, so sampling an unsorted set is not reproducible even with a fixed seed.

The rest of the function is unchanged — it already iterates `sorted(candidate_ticks)`.

## Stage 3 — Config change, regenerate, retrain

**Depends on Stage 2.** Running this first produces a *worse* model — see §2.2.

### 3.1 Threshold feasibility

A window can only reach ratio `r` if the interval is at least `r × window_sec`
long. With `window_sec = 2.0` and the 40 measured swing intervals
(min 1.50 s):

| Threshold | Needs interval ≥ | Usable intervals |
|---|---|---|
| 0.70 | 1.40 s | **40 / 40** |
| 0.75 | 1.50 s | 40 / 40 — but the shortest yields exactly one window, at exactly 0.75 |
| 0.80 | 1.60 s | 39 / 40 |

**Use 0.70.** It clears all 40 with margin; 0.75 leaves the shortest interval on
a floating-point knife edge in the tick arithmetic of
[`_add_window_start_range()`](action/generate_dataset.py#L812).

### 3.2 Edit `dataset_config.yaml`

Replace the `windowing` block ([lines 23–30](dataset_config.yaml#L23-L30)):

```yaml
windowing:
  include_unlabeled_as_other: true      # was false — unlocks 55.6 min of negatives
  max_background_windows: 1200          # new — caps generation cost and class balance

  swing_stride_sec: 0.15
  swing_overlap_threshold: 0.70         # was 0.30
  swing_ignore_below: 0.10              # new — below this, eligible as negative

  walk_stride_sec: 0.25
  walk_overlap_threshold: 0.50          # unchanged — walk overlaps are already clean
  walk_ignore_below: 0.10               # new

  other_stride_sec: 0.5
  other_overlap_threshold: 0.50         # unchanged
```

Leave `sampling`, `labels`, `priority`, and `pose` untouched. Do **not** change
`window_sec` or `target_fps` — see [§5](#5-rejected-shortening-the-window-to-10-s).

# Phase 2 — Deferred refinements

**Do not start until Stage 4 records a verdict.** Each of these was scoped
during planning and deliberately deferred; the detail is preserved so it can be
picked up without re-deriving it.

### Stage A — Impact anchors (fixes Leak B)

Derive the impact instant from the wrist-speed peak inside each existing labeled
interval, so loose 3 s spans become precise instants without hand-relabeling all
40. Reuse `left/right_wrist_relative_speed` from
[`action/preprocessing.py`](action/preprocessing.py#L200). **Smooth before
argmax** — raw keypoint jitter produces single-frame spikes. Reject peaks landing
within 0.2 s of the search boundary (real impact is probably outside the label)
or with low prominence. Output `data/annotations/swing_anchors.json`. Spot-check
10 by eye against [`write_debug_video()`](action/generate_dataset.py#L696) before
trusting the rest.

Side benefit: anchors are impact events, so they give a *free* event-labeled
tuning set on the existing validation-split videos — no extra labeling, and it
keeps `data/hold_out/` untouched for final measurement.

### Stage B — Phase-aligned window generation

Replace overlap thresholding for `swing` with anchor-relative placement. A swing
is not symmetric about impact (backswing ~1.0 s, downswing ~0.25 s, follow-through
~0.5 s), so impact should sit at ~65 % through the window, not centered:

```
core           = [anchor − 1.0, anchor + 0.5]        # must be fully contained
target_start   = anchor − 1.3                        # impact at 1.3s of 2.0s
positive_start ∈ target_start ± 0.25                 # stride 0.05, ~11 windows/swing
```

Windows clear of the core by ≥ 0.25 s and within 3.0 s of it become **boundary
hard negatives** — the pre-address and post-shot idle, which are the exact
confusers. Tag them (`negative_kind: "boundary"`) so they can be upweighted.
Also makes live detection latency deterministic, since the inference window is
trailing (§1.5).

### Stage C — Inference debouncing

A `SwingEventDebouncer` beside
[`ActionSequenceBuffer`](action/live_buffer.py#L8), consumed in
[`update_live_action()`](action/runtime.py#L44). Keep classifier output raw —
debouncing is a decision-layer concern.

| Transition | Rule |
|---|---|
| Enter swing | ≥ 3 of last 4 windows above `enter_threshold` |
| Stay in swing | ≥ 1 of last 4 above `exit_threshold` |
| Refractory | suppress new events for 3.0 s after emitting |

The refractory period is nearly free and kills duplicate detections outright: a
golfer does not swing twice in three seconds. Once this exists, **lower
`class_thresholds.swing` from 0.90** — it is currently doing debouncing's job
badly, suppressing per-window noise by demanding extreme per-window confidence
and costing recall on genuine swings.

### Stage D — Detection-metric harness

Automate Stage 4. Extend `RealisticVideoRun` to emit `(timestamp, confidence)`
per event; add `action/evaluate_detection.py` matching events to labels with a
±1.0 s window, greedy nearest-first. Report **FP per minute of idle** (the
failing product metric), impact recall, and median detection latency — not
window accuracy or macro-F1, which are dominated by how many windows the
generator happened to emit.

Run it on two tiers: Stage A's anchors over validation-split videos for
parameter tuning, and hand-labeled `data/hold_out/` read **once** for the
reported number. Tuning on the holdout would make the final figure optimistic.

### Stage E — Priority margin fix

In [`assign_label_by_overlap()`](action/generate_dataset.py#L590), require a
priority winner to also beat the runner-up, so 0.31 swing cannot outrank 0.69
`other`. Largely subsumed by Stage 2's ignore band at a 0.70 threshold, but
still correct in principle and cheap.

### Stage F — More data

40 swing intervals — **2.0 minutes of swing across 79 minutes of footage** — is
the binding constraint. Every technique here trades sample count for sample
quality, which is right at this contamination level, but none of it creates
data. Collecting more swings, labeled as impact events from the start, dominates
every other item in this document. Note the asymmetry: idle is abundant and now
usable after Stage 3, so a shooting session should prioritise **swings**.

## 5. Rejected: shortening the window to 1.0 s

Considered and rejected. Recorded so it is not revisited.

- A full swing is ~1.2–1.7 s, so a 1.0 s window **structurally cannot contain
  one**. The model would see fragments: a backswing-only crop resembles a
  stretch, a follow-through-only crop resembles a turn-and-walk-off. Trades idle
  false positives for a harder set.
- The three `MaxPool1d(2)` blocks in
  [`TemporalPoseCNN`](action/classifier.py#L25) take 30 → 15 → 7 → 3. At 15
  frames that becomes 15 → 7 → 3 → 1, collapsing the flatten to a single
  temporal position. Would force an architecture change.
- **The compute argument does not hold.** `TemporalPoseCNN` is ~370 k params and
  ~2.5 M MACs per call; `yolo26s-pose.pt` costs three to four orders of magnitude
  more and is paid **per frame regardless of window length**. Raising
  `classify_stride_sec` from 0.25 → 0.10 costs essentially nothing at inference.
  Finer stride only costs during offline dataset generation, in
  [`populate_pose_cache()`](action/generate_dataset.py#L499).

Window length was never the cause. The acceptance rule was.
